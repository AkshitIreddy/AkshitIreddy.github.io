## Applied decisions

- Keep headline words in normal document flow. React with one compositor-only
  transform on the final highlighted phrase instead of measuring and moving
  every glyph under the cursor.
- Preserve the expected text cursor/arrow behavior; interaction should not turn
  the headline into a targeting surface.
- Keep motion bounded, settle it promptly, and disable it under reduced motion.
- Use a responsive spacing scale with larger title-to-copy gaps and readable
  paragraph leading rather than isolated one-off padding fixes.
- Keep body explanations in short, structured blocks with a comfortable line
  length. Decorative labels can remain compact, but project meaning cannot be
  hidden in tiny display type.
- Set every portfolio video to `playbackRate = 1`; retain the tutorial source's
  native 30 fps cadence when making the shipping loop.
- Use the complete Alcove README animation rather than the previous 15-second
  editorial excerpt.
- Use a two-color focus treatment that remains visible across the paper,
  violet, and near-black room materials.
- Keep strong shape, border, label, and state signifiers on interactive cards;
  color alone is not the interaction cue.
- Prepare a GitHub Pages workflow that assembles only public runtime files.
  Publishing remains a separate explicit action.
