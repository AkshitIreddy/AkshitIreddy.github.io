01 · recent habitat / 2026

Alcove

A local-first notebook built like a shelf of storybooks—not a stack of files. Pages turn, covers become places, and the library never leaves your machine. One tool in the rail is optional: an AI agent that answers questions and drafts pages for your review.

  • local-first
  • Rust + Tauri
  • page physics
  • optional AI agent
  • no account

catalogue card · the optional agent

One optional tool in the book's left rail, run with your own Cohere key. Ask a question and get an answer; ask for pages and they are drafted away from your book, rendered as real Alcove pages and checked before you see them. The draft stays a preview until you choose Insert—and every other book stays local.
