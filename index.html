01 · recent habitat / 2026

Alcove

A local-first notebook built like a shelf of storybooks—not a stack of files. Pages turn, covers become places, and the library never leaves your machine. When you want help drafting, an optional AI agent can answer questions and propose pages for your review.

  • local-first
  • Rust + Tauri
  • page physics
  • kept not stored
  • no account

catalogue card · kept, not stored

Every book lives on your shelf with real page physics—covers become places, leaves turn with weight, and nothing syncs to a cloud account. The catalogue is a local index of what you own: pull a volume, read it in place, and close it back onto the plank.

AI Desktop Pet

field note · the desktop habitat

A conversational desktop character with window awareness, screen vision, and long-term memory. It wanders the screen, climbs window edges, chases the cursor, joins other pets, and turns spoken Convai conversations into body language of its own.

03 · playable light / recent work

Keyscape

A Rust/Tauri per-key RGB engine for ASUS ROG laptops. Fifty hand-built effects turn the keyboard into a programmable landscape of ripples, weather, particles, and color.

  • Rust + Tauri
  • 50 effects
  • per-key RGB

One press · or hit R

The instrument answers: light leaves the keyboard and rolls across the room — near surfaces flare first, far corners catch it late and faint.
